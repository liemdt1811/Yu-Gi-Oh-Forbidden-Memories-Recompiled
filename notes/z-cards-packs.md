# Z mod: card packs

2 packs, sold on the Password screen behind the triangle button (`mods/z/packs.json`, made by `tools/pc/real_cards/list_packs.py` from `packs.json`). Every pack costs 50 starchips and opens when the save has beaten the duelist named under *Unlocks*; a locked pack is listed face down with its condition. ATK in brackets.

- Price: 50 starchips; 9 cards
- Unlocks: beat **Simon Muran**
- Slots: 8 of the common tier, then one by the odds rare 76%, super 15%, ultra 8%, secret 1%
